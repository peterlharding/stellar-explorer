# Stellar Explorer 0.1.0

The first release: the nearby stars in 3D, to look around and travel among, on the Mac, the Apple Vision Pro, and Windows with an HTC Vive Pro 2.
It is still an early prototype, built and run from the source tree; there are no downloadable builds yet.

## Highlights

- **113,012 stars at their true positions and colours**, most of them within a few hundred parsecs of the Sun, loaded in about a second (all platforms). Travel stops at 1500 parsecs, where the catalogue has all but run out.
- **Pointing at a star names it** on a panel: by hand on the Vision Pro, by controller on the Vive, by mouse on the desktop. Keep a star to hold its details in view.
- **Travel by voice** on the Mac and the Vision Pro, with Apple's on-device recogniser: "Stellar, go to Sirius", "Stellar, go there" to the star pointed at, "back off", "closer", "return to the Sun", "galactic north".
- **Travel without speaking** on the Vision Pro, from an actions list on the panel; the panel can be carried around by its grab bar.
- **A desktop console** in the app's window: the position and the star kept, a command box, buttons for the commands, and the log.
  It can also steer the Vision Pro over the local network and follow where the wearer is looking. "Quit the headset app" closes the headset's app from the desk.
- **Blender-style mouse navigation** on the desktop: middle-drag to orbit the kept star, Shift + middle-drag to pan, the wheel to zoom, drag to look around, click a star to keep it.
- On the desktop, stars keep their colour close up and grow as they are approached.
- The version and build are shown in the window title, the console and the headset's panel.

## Data

- Built from the [HYG Database](https://codeberg.org/astronexus/hyg) 4.4 (CC BY-SA 4.0), [Hipparcos, the New Reduction](https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/viz-bin/cat/I/311) (van Leeuwen 2007) for positions and distances, and the [NASA Exoplanet Archive](https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/) Planetary Systems Composite Parameters, retrieved 2026-10-25 (6,451 planets).
- Build the catalogue with `make catalogue` before the first run. It carries schema 1, and the app refuses a catalogue of any other schema, saying which one it found.
- The catalogue covers stars bright enough to see from Earth, so it thins out with distance: from far out, the sky is crowded towards the Sun and nearly empty away from it.
- There is no `user.sqlite` yet.

## Under the hood

- One version for the repository, kept in step by `make version-set`, including the Mac and visionOS bundle versions.
- The engine-independent C++ core (catalogue, astrometry, voice matching) is a static library behind a C ABI, interface version 4. Rebuild it for each platform after pulling (see [src/core/README.md](https://github.com/peterlharding/stellar-explorer/blob/v0.1.0/src/core/README.md)); the app quits with an error on a stale build.
- The full list is in the [changelog](https://github.com/peterlharding/stellar-explorer/blob/v0.1.0/CHANGELOG.md).

## Not tested

- This release's own build was built, packaged and run on the Mac, and packaged for the Vision Pro, but not run in either headset. The features were tested in the Vision Pro during development, up to the build of 3 November 2026.
- Windows and the Vive were last tested on 2 November 2026, before voice, the console and the new mouse navigation. Voice is not available on Windows.
- The headset frame rates have not been measured against the full star budget for this release.
