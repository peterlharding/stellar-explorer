# Stellar Explorer 0.2.1

A small change for the Apple Vision Pro: the pointing ray from your hand is gone, as look and pinch now does everything it did.

## Highlights

- **No hand ray** (Vision Pro): look at a star and pinch to keep it; look at the kept star and pinch twice to let it go.
  Your hand's pinch no longer acts twice, once for the ray and once for the look, which could let a kept star go just as you kept another.
- The panel says "Look at a star and pinch" until you keep one.
- Anyone who wants the ray back can set `bHandRay=True` in `Config/DefaultGame.ini`, or start the app with `-HandRay`.
- The Vive's controllers keep their ray, and nothing changes on the desktop.

## Data

- No change: the catalogue is the same as in 0.2.0, schema 1, and need not be rebuilt.

## Under the hood

- The core is unchanged, at interface version 5.
- The full list is in the [changelog](https://github.com/peterlharding/stellar-explorer/blob/v0.2.1/CHANGELOG.md).

## Not tested

- Tested in the Vision Pro on 5 November 2026 with this change; this release's own build, differing only in its version, was packaged for the Vision Pro but not run in it. Letting a star go by pinching twice was not tried in the headset.
- Windows and the Vive were not built or run for this release. As for 0.2.0, the Windows core library must be rebuilt for interface version 5 before the app will start there.
- The headset frame rates have not been measured against the full star budget for this release.
