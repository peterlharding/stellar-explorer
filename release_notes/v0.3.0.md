# Stellar Explorer 0.3.0

See just the space around you: hide every star beyond a distance you choose, and widen or narrow it as you go.
On the Apple Vision Pro it is set by a slider on the hand menu, by stretching with both hands, or by voice; on the desktop by a slider in the console.

## Highlights

- **A radius of visibility** (all platforms): only the stars within a chosen distance of you are shown, those near the edge fading, and the radius travels with you.
  Stars beyond it cannot be pointed at or kept by look and pinch.
- **Set it by hand** (Vision Pro): a slider under the hand menu's buttons, touched with a fingertip, or looked at, pinched and slid; or pinch with both hands and move them apart to see further out, or together to see less.
  The far end of the slider shows every star.
- **Set it by voice** (Mac and Vision Pro): "Stellar, show within 20 light years", "show more" (twice as far), "show less" (half as far), "show everything".
  Light years are the surest; parsecs work too ("show within 6 parsecs", or "6 PC"), though Apple's recogniser often mishears the word, and the app reads the commonest mishearings as parsecs.
  Every answer gives both units: "Stars within 20 ly (6.1 pc)".
- **Set it from the desk** (Mac): a slider and Show more, Show less and Show everything buttons in the console; steering the Vision Pro, the console shows the same stars as the headset.
- **Quit by voice** (Mac and Vision Pro): "Stellar, quit" or "Stellar, exit", then "quit" again within 8 seconds.
- **A proper start screen** (Vision Pro): the window shown while the app starts now reads "Stellar Explorer" with the version and build, instead of Unreal's developer text.

## Fixed

- The desktop console took a minute or more to quit after the headset app had closed; it now quits at once.
- On the headset's panel, a long branch name ran past the frame.

## Data

- No change: the catalogue is the same as in 0.2.1, schema 1, and need not be rebuilt.
- There is no `user.sqlite` yet.

## Under the hood

- The engine-independent C++ core is at interface version 6, for the new voice commands. Rebuild the core libraries after pulling: `scripts/make_macos.sh` on a Mac, and `build-msvc` on Windows. The app quits with an error on a stale build.
- The star material has a new `ViewRadius` parameter: run `./spike.sh material` to regenerate it before building.
- The full list is in the [changelog](https://github.com/peterlharding/stellar-explorer/blob/v0.3.0/CHANGELOG.md).

## Not tested

- Tested in the Vision Pro on 5 November 2026 up to the last change but one; the last, two wider rules for reading "parsecs" as heard, is covered by the core's tests but was not tried in the headset. This release's own build, differing only in its version, was packaged for the Vision Pro but not run in it.
- Windows and the Vive were not built or run for this release. The Windows core library must be rebuilt for interface version 6 before the app will start there, and the Vive has no slider or two-hand stretch for the radius yet.
- The headset frame rates have not been measured against the full star budget for this release.
