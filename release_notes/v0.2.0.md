# Stellar Explorer 0.2.0

New ways to drive the app on the Apple Vision Pro without speaking or pointing: look at what you want and pinch, as everywhere else in visionOS, and a menu on your wrist.
The panel describing a star can now be hidden, moved and brought back, and it gets out of the way by itself.

## Highlights

- **Look and pinch** (Vision Pro): look at a star and pinch to keep it, or look at a button and pinch to press it.
  Look at the panel or its bar, pinch, and move your hand to move the panel, as you would a visionOS window.
- **A hand menu** (Vision Pro): turn the back of your left wrist towards you and touch the button on it with your right index finger.
  A column of buttons opens beside it: Go there, Back off, Closer, Return to the Sun, Let go, and Hide info.
  Press them by touching them or by look and pinch; move the menu by the bar under it, and close it with the ✕ beside the bar or the wrist button.
- **Hide the panel** with the ✕ beside its bar (Vision Pro and Vive), "Hide info" on the menus and in the console, or by saying "Stellar, hide info".
  "Show info" brings it back. In a headset the panel also goes by itself after 3 seconds with no star pointed at or kept.
- **Quicker voice** (Mac and Vision Pro): a command is acted on 0.4 seconds after you finish it, even with other speech or a video playing nearby.
- On the Vision Pro the "..." button above the panel is gone, replaced by the hand menu. On the Vive it stays, with Hide info added to its list.

## Fixed

- A desktop console could not reach the app for about half a minute after the app was restarted.
- The build line on the headset's panel ran past its frame.

## Data

- No change: the catalogue is the same as in 0.1.0, schema 1, and need not be rebuilt.
- There is no `user.sqlite` yet.

## Under the hood

- The engine-independent C++ core is at interface version 5, for the hide and show info commands. Rebuild the core libraries after pulling: `scripts/make_macos.sh` on a Mac (which now builds them when run), and `build-msvc` on Windows. The app quits with an error on a stale build.
- Look and pinch uses visionOS's spatial events, which Unreal Engine 5.8 does not ask for, through a small Swift file compiled into the app.
- The full list is in the [changelog](https://github.com/peterlharding/stellar-explorer/blob/v0.2.0/CHANGELOG.md).

## Not tested

- Look and pinch, the hand menu, hiding the panel and the quicker voice were tested in the Vision Pro on 5 November 2026, up to the last change; this release's own build, differing only in its version, was packaged for the Vision Pro but not run in it.
- Windows and the Vive were not built or run for this release. The Windows core library must be rebuilt for interface version 5 before the app will start there.
- The headset frame rates have not been measured against the full star budget for this release.
