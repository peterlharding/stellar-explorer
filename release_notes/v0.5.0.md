# Stellar Explorer 0.5.0

This release is for the Vive. The headset's controllers now reach the controls that the Vision Pro's hands already did: a menu you can drive without aiming at the panel, a visible wand in each hand, and a dial for how far out stars are shown.

## Highlights

- **Drive the actions menu from the controller (Vive).** Press the right controller's menu button to open the list of commands - Go there, Back off, Closer, Return to the Sun, Let go, Hide info, Quit. Scroll it with the trackpad and choose with the trackpad's click, or point the ray at an item and click. Quit is now on the menu, so you no longer have to hold both triggers to leave.
- **A wand in each hand (Vive).** A thin rod sits along each controller, with the ray running on from its tip, so it is easy to tell which hand is which and where it points. The rods are colour-coded like a ship's navigation lights: the left hand red for port, the right green for starboard.
- **A view-radius dial (Vive).** The left controller's menu button or grip opens a panel, opposite the star panel, with a slider showing how far out stars are shown. Slide it with the left trackpad - it eases for fine control and holds still when you lift your thumb - and click the trackpad to show every star or return to your last radius. This is the Vive's way to the radius of visibility that the Vision Pro reaches through its hand slider and voice.

## Fixed

- The opening prompts on the star panel ("Pull the trigger", and the like) no longer run past the right edge of the frame.

## Data

- No change to the catalogue. The database from 0.3.0 onward still opens; nothing needs rebuilding or downloading again.

## Under the hood

- All of the above is in the Unreal front end; the core library is unchanged, so it does not need rebuilding after this release.

## Not tested

- The Vive changes were run on the Vive. The desktop and Vision Pro builds are unaffected by them.
